# Known limits

- **Models:** Scripted tests establish contracts and recovery behavior, not live-provider reliability. Real DeepSeek checks have covered selected extraction and workflow examples on synthetic data. Other providers need separate live acceptance. Model failures and verified fallback text are distinct from successful semantic interpretation.
- **Language and requirements:** The interface and replies use English. Offline parsing has a limited grammar. Unsupported budget ceilings, exact quantity splits, arbitrary quality thresholds, and complex negations may require clarification. Grounding checks can reject otherwise understandable free-form wording.
- **Order identity:** Lookup requires an exact supported order ID; customer names do not select an order. Allocation does not edit master data. Manual or conversational registration supports separately reviewed corrections only for unstarted READY/UNASSIGNED orders without completed pieces or active allocations.
- **Production:** Completion, cancellation, lapse, and reassignment are supported. Restoring a lapsed order, automatic completion, and contacting workshops are not implemented. Cancellation preserves recorded completed quantities.
- **Dates and queues:** Business dates are explicit and do not advance automatically. FIFO reservations decay in calendar days; predicted empty queues do not prove physical completion. Work calendars, random rework, and operation-level scheduling are not integrated into live production. Hard deadlines block late plans; ordinary due dates permit warnings. Automatic lapse policy is unresolved.
- **Optimization:** The planner optimizes one order over its supported workshop subsets and integer breakpoints, not future orders globally. `hybrid`, `weighted_hybrid`, and the original deadline-gated comparison score have different meanings. Candidate lists are capped, not exhaustive lists of every integer split. Large batches and workshop sets increase computation; supported imports cap workshops at eight.
- **Splits and preference:** The base native LLM allocation flow supports one workshop. Offline mode and the Allocator support authorized splits for supported objectives. `weighted_hybrid` remains single-workshop. A named required/preferred workshop is a hard sole-workshop constraint; soft preference is not implemented. Alternative selection does not authorize production.
- **Freshness:** Confirmation recalculates from current facts. Changed order versions or plans require review. Existing sessions retain their business date, mode, and model; create another session to change those selections.
- **Explanations:** Checks cover identifiers, dates, supported numbers, and selected contradictions. They do not prove every sentence or associate every number with its intended entity. Saved structured evidence remains authoritative; generated prose still needs review.
- **Evaluation:** Bundled orders and request labels are development data, not an independent holdout. Historical R02/R13 labels differ from current clarification behavior. R16 and R14 have documented annotation/policy ambiguity. Simulator metrics do not establish natural-language accuracy, explanation faithfulness, split benefits, or operational gains.
- **Deployment and access:** The supported deployment is one local process/worker on `127.0.0.1` with SQLite. Accounts share a workspace; session ownership is not tenant isolation. Public hosting, distributed execution, global provider throttling, and concurrency/load acceptance are not implemented.
- **Audit:** Historical actors and missing imported milestones are not invented. `local-demo` is the unauthenticated demonstration identity. A failed database can prevent even a failure audit from being saved; retain the returned error. Backups contain account and conversation data and must remain private.
- **Data compatibility:** The external Data Schema2 DDL is not present, so equivalence to that unspecified schema has not been established. CSV import does not import external assignments, partial completions, or audit history.

See [current implementation](docs/current_architecture.md), [testing](docs/testing.md), and [deployment](docs/deployment.md) for the corresponding controls and verification procedures.
